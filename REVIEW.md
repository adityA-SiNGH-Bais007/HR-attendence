List every defect you found in the starter `app/main.py` (helpers and endpoints). For each one:

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `compute_late_minutes` / lines 36–37 | Incorrect 10-minute grace period boundary condition (R2). Checked `minutes > 10` after integer floor, returning 0 at 10 min 1 sec. | Shift 09:30, punch-in at 09:40:01 returned 0 instead of 10. | Checked `elapsed_seconds > 600`, returning `int(elapsed_seconds // 60)`. |
| 2 | `compute_late_minutes` / lines 34–35 | Overnight shift failure (R1). Replaces hour on punch-in date rather than anchoring shift start to attendance date. | Overnight shift 22:00–06:00, punch-in next day at 00:15 returned 0 late minutes instead of 135. | Anchored `shift_start_dt` to the attendance date in IST: `datetime(year, month, day, h, m, tzinfo=IST)`. |
| 3 | `compute_work_hours` / line 41 | Used Python `round()` (banker's / round-half-to-even) instead of decimal half-up rounding (R4). | Duration of 2.625 hours rounded to 2.62 instead of 2.63. | Used Python `Decimal` with `ROUND_HALF_UP` quantized to `0.01`. |
| 4 | `compute_overtime` / line 47 | Missing minimum 30-minute threshold (R3). Returned any positive minute difference. | Punch-out 15 minutes after shift end returned 15 instead of 0. | Added threshold check: `minutes if minutes >= 30 else 0`. |
| 5 | `compute_overtime` / lines 45–46 | Failed to handle overnight shifts (R1). | 6 | `compute_overtime` / lines 46–47 | Subtraction of offset-naive and offset-aware datetimes raises `TypeError`. | Passing naive PyMongo datetime to `compute_overtime` crashes with 500 `TypeError`. | Normalized all datetimes to UTC/IST timezone-aware before subtraction. |

| 8 | `punch_in` / line 103 | Failed to determine overnight shift attendance date (R1). | Overnight shift 22:00–06:00, punch-in at 01:00 IST received today's date instead of yesterday's. | When `shift_end <= shift_start` and punch time `< shift_end`, subtracted one day from IST date. |

Also note anything you looked at and decided was **not** a defect, and why:
- **`os.getenv` with fallback defaults:** Reading `MONGO_URI` and `MONGO_DB` via `os.getenv` with fallback to localhost defaults is standard and allows environment variables to take priority as required.
- **`status_code=201` on creation endpoints:** `POST /employees` and `POST /attendance/punch-in` returning HTTP 201 Created adheres to REST standards and the OpenAPI specification.
- **`load_dotenv()` inclusion:** Calling `load_dotenv()` allows local development with `.env` files while allowing injected system environment variables to override them.
